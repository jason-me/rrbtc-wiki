# MiningLucky

MiningLucky is a Bitcoin hashrate rental experience operated by Carlos Monzon through Power CM Software. It lets a user direct a time limited SHA 256 mining session to a Bitcoin reward address without owning mining hardware.

## How It Works

The user chooses available power and duration, provides a Bitcoin reward address, and receives a session view with assigned power, accepted shares, best share, activity, and timing. The illustrations explain the process and do not represent hardware ownership.

## Reward Allocation

If a session produces a block that Bitcoin Core accepts, 99.6 percent of the accepted block base reward and transaction fees is assigned to the user's reward address. MiningLucky retains 0.4 percent.

## Evidence

Paid sessions expose the assigned power, submitted work, best share, recent activity, and elapsed time. These measurements show service delivery and mining activity. They do not prove that a future block will be found.

## Risk

Mining is probabilistic. A rental can complete with no block and no reward. There is no guarantee of profit or recovery of the rental price.
