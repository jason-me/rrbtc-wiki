# Bitcoin After BIP-110 — Where Do We Go From Here?

**Date:** 2026-09-10
**Presenter:** Joshua Lowrance
**Topics:** bip-110, chain split, proof-of-work, blake2b, consensus

**Slides:** /img/slides/2026-09-10-bitcoin-after-bip-110/slide_000.jpg … slide_037.jpg

---

## Outline & Notes

On September 1, 2026, a chain split became real: two networks now both claim to be Bitcoin after the activation of BIP-110's proof-of-work change from SHA-256 to BLAKE2b. This talk walks through how the community got here, what the split means for miners, holders, and node operators, and where things go from here.
