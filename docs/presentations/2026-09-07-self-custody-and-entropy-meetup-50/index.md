**Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Presenter:** RRBTC  
**Topics:** custody, entropy, diy signing devices, inheritance  

**Deck:** [SpeakerDeck](https://speakerdeck.com/rrbtc/round-rock-bitcoiners-number-50-on-self-custody-and-entropy)

## Outline & Notes

Community discussion on self-custody tradeoffs and entropy, framed by the July 30 incident as a reminder of the cost of custody mistakes. Covers custody models, seed generation and storage, and DIY signing devices.

## DIY Signing Devices Covered

- [Krux](/entities/krux) — open-source firmware for off-the-shelf microcontrollers
- [SeedSigner](/entities/seedsigner) — Raspberry Pi Zero-based air-gapped signer
- [Jade DIY](/entities/jade-diy) — Blockstream Jade firmware on generic hardware
- [Specter DIY](/entities/specter-diy) — Specter DIY air-gapped hardware wallet
